About Us

We are La Grâce Law Firm

Every client is different, and our approach to work reflects that. We’re an independent, nimble firm of driven, passionate, entrepreneurial people who are dedicated to your success. When you partner with La Grâce Law Firm, you get a tailored-to-you team that prioritizes your goals and never loses sight of them. This is why we’re trusted by some of the biggest companies and most creative startups on some of their most significant files.

We embrace new ways of thinking and dig deeper to understand your risk tolerance, needs, and objectives. Whether you're a startup or industry titan (or somewhere in between), we'll take your business where it needs to go. We guide our clients through difficult issues, bringing our insight and judgment to each situation. Our innovative approaches create original solutions to our clients' most complex domestic and multijurisdictional deals and disputes. By thinking on behalf of our clients every day, we anticipate what they want, provide what they need and build lasting relationships. We do what it takes to help our clients achieve their ambitions.

We value the perspectives and ideas that come from people with diverse backgrounds and experiences. Our Team works to strengthen inclusion across our practice areas. They're also responsible for diversity-related initiatives at the firm.

We care about each other and the world around us. These are the concepts that shape our distinctive culture and differentiate us from others. They capture the unique spirit of our Firm, and guide the behaviors that enable us to deliver on the promises we make to our clients and our people.

Why Choose Us
