About Us

A Legacy of Legal Excellence

Our team of experienced Attorneys have a diverse range of expertise, covering a wide spectrum of legal areas. We specialize in International Trade Laws, Company Law, Commercial Law, Estate Litigation, etc.

Our Attorneys possess sound knowledge of the law and superior advocacy skills, enjoying the finest reputation, and being briefed in many high profile and important cases. Members of La Grâce Law Firm provide services of the highest quality and work collaboratively with those that instruct them. Our firm is conveniently located in Istanbul as well as Quebec and our members are available to provide all aspects of legal advice both within and outside of the court setting.
